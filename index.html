Company 关于公司

A trading house that
does its own documentation. 一家自己做单据的
贸易公司。

Qianyan Holding (Shenzhen) Group Co., Ltd. is a Sino-foreign joint venture headquartered in Shenzhen, with a Hong Kong desk for settlement and documentation. Our remit is international bulk commodity trade and supply-chain services — covering metals & precious metals, minerals, energy & chemicals, fertilizers, and agricultural goods. 乾衍控股（深圳）集团有限公司，总部位于深圳，香港设立结算与单证办公室。我们做国际大宗商品贸易与供应链服务——覆盖金属与贵金属、矿产品、能源化工、化肥及农副产品。

The desk exists to keep the boring parts of cross-border trade working. Origin paperwork, lab certificates where issued, port-side logistics, and a counter-party you can email on a weekday morning. 我们的工作，是把跨境贸易里那些不耀眼的部分做好：产地单据、检测报告、港口物流，以及一个工作日早晨能发邮件联系上的交易对手。
